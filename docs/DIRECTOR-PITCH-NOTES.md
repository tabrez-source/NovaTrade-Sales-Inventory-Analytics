# NovaTrade — business pitch notes

## 60-second explanation

NovaTrade is an independent sales and inventory analytics proof of concept built with synthetic data. The Executive Overview answers five plain business questions: How much was sold? How many units and orders produced that value? Which products, regions and sales leaders drove the result? How much business occurred outside assigned sales regions? And where should management investigate next?

The figures are illustrative. A real pilot would connect the same decision structure to a company's authorized data sources, agree the KPI definitions with management, reconcile the totals, and establish refresh, security and ownership rules.

## Three questions for a nontechnical director

1. Which three sales or inventory decisions do you need to make every week?
2. Which exceptions currently take the longest to identify—weak products, regional gaps, distributor performance or stock movement?
3. Which system or file contains the trusted sales, target and inventory figures for a small pilot?

## Safe positioning

> NovaTrade is an independent portfolio project using synthetic data. It demonstrates how a comparable product-distribution business could monitor sales and inventory. A customized solution would begin only after reviewing authorized data and agreeing the business definitions.

## Do not claim

- Do not describe the synthetic figures as a real company's revenue, orders or stock.
- Do not imply that NovaTrade is affiliated with, approved by or built for a named company.
- Do not call it a production deployment until real sources, security, refresh, ownership and reconciliation are approved.
